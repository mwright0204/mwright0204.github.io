Benin Bronze Experience

The Benin Bronze Experience gives children and young people the chance to learn the sacred tradition of bronze casting from the Kingdom of Benin. With the expert VR guidance of your mentor - an experienced bronze caster - you will be immersed in the Benin City of the 1500s where you will learn the traditional West-African lost-wax bronze casting process.
